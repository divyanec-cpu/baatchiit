About BaatChiit.ai

Bol ke dekho, samajh sabko aayega.

BaatChiit lets two people who don't share a language talk to each other using one phone. Put the phone between you: the screen is split in two, the far half is upside down so it reads correctly for the person across from you, and each side speaks into their own microphone button. What one person says appears — and is spoken aloud — in the other person's language.

It supports eleven Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia and English. In most cases you don't have to choose — each side's language is recognised the first time that person speaks.

How it works

Speech recognition, translation and the spoken reply are all provided by Sarvam AI, an Indian company building language models for Indian languages. When you hold the microphone, that recording is sent to Sarvam's service, translated, and the result comes back as text and audio.

What is stored, and what isn't

Kept on our server:

  • Your mobile number — it is how you sign in.
  • Your minutes balance and how many translations you've done.
  • Anything you chose to add in the profile: name, city, and what you use the app for. All of that is optional and can be left blank.

Not kept by us:

  • Your conversations. The history list lives on your own phone and never leaves it — clear your browser data and it is gone.
  • Your voice recordings. Audio is passed to Sarvam AI for translation and is not stored by us afterwards.

We don't sell data, show adverts, or share your number with anyone else. Sending an SMS one-time password uses MSG91, so your number passes through them to deliver that message.

Minutes and payment

New accounts get 15 minutes free. Roughly ten exchanges use one minute. Paid top-ups are not switched on yet — when they are, payment will be handled by Razorpay and this page will say so.

Deleting your account

Email support@baatchiit.com from any address, telling us the mobile number you signed in with, and the account and its details will be removed.

Contact

Questions, problems, or a translation that came out wrong — support@baatchiit.com. Corrections to the wording in any language are especially welcome.
